# Master Federal Court Complaint - Guam

**Plaintiff:** People of Guam
**Defendant:** United States; DoD; DOI; Congress
**Jurisdiction:** 28 USC 1331, 1343; Territorial Clause; UN Charter Ch. XI

## Causes of Action
1. **Denial of Self-Determination** — UN Charter Art. 73, decolonization
2. **Military Land Taking** — Eminent domain without just compensation
3. **Political Disenfranchisement** — No vote for President, no voting Congress rep
4. **Environmental Contamination** — PFAS, UXO, nuclear legacy, Superfund
5. **Economic Colonization** — Extraction economy, federal dependency
6. **Cultural Erasure** — Indigenous language/land suppression

## Historical Arc: 1898-2026
## Judgment: 1898 Spanish-American War, 1941-44 Japanese Occupation, 1950 Organic Act, 1/3 Land US Military, Chamorro Land Takings, Nuclear Downwinders, Tip of Spear
## Pool: $50.00B

## Demands
A. Self-determination plebiscite (Statehood/Independence/Free Association)
B. Military land return / fair rent / cleanup
C. Federal voting rights (House delegate vote, presidential vote)
D. Environmental remediation (PFAS, UXO, radiation, Superfund)
E. Economic diversification funding
F. Cultural preservation (language, land, traditional governance)
